How to play

The journey. Five floating islands climb the sky along the Coil, fifteen levels each. Win a level to open the next. Every island starts at its home base and ends at a bridge up to the next one.

Winning. Every level wants something different: apples, a score, a long snake, a minute alive, keys and a gate, pressure plates and a seal, a thief to corner, or a relic hidden in an endless maze. The map says which, and a plate beside the board keeps count. Every level also has a challenge, something extra to do (smash three boulders, win without a scratch) for a few points, coins or a gem.

Bosses. Levels 5, 10 and 15 are fights with the island's boss. When its head crashes into your snake it hurts itself; when your head runs into it, you get hurt. Swords add to every blow, and fireballs and force fields hurt it too, unless it shrugs that spell off. The first fight knocks the wind out of it, the second breaks its silver armor, the third takes its red hearts. While it's worn out you can bite its body.

Shopkeepers. Each boss you beat opens a stop off the path: a merchant who sells one kind of thing, a challenger with a quest of three trials and a prize, then the bank and the bridge.

Your kit. One gear and one spell. Gear wears out and a spell (Space) runs out of charges. One rune is active at a time, under your hearts: it's yours for good, with a good side and a bad side, and it comes broken, cracked, fine or pristine. Snacks go in your pocket, four at most: press 1 to 4 to eat one. A familiar comes along beside you and never wears out. Every item has a strength and a weakness. Choose what to carry before each level.

Money. Each island has its own money for its own upgrade tree, and every upgrade works on every island. Banks trade money down to the island before, or into gems for skins and themes.

Home base. The tree on every island. Click the carving on its trunk for upgrades, the satchel for your kit, the trophy for achievements, and the mirror or the easel for skins and themes.

About

An original game. The story, art, soundtrack and narration were all made for it. Thank you for playing.

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD: steer the snake (either can be turned off in Settings)

Space: cast your spell during a level, or play again after one

1 to 4: eat the snack in that pocket slot (or click it beside the board)

Esc or P: pause

Touch: swipe on the board or use the on-screen pad; its Spell button casts

The map: drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom; up close, the arrows step between islands. Click a stop to fly in: its button is right there, the left and right arrow keys walk the path, and Esc flies back out

Menus: arrow keys move the highlight, Space or Enter selects, Esc goes back

How to play

The journey. Five islands, fifteen levels each, strung together by the Coil. Win a level to open the next. Levels 5, 10 and 15 are boss fights, and each one opens something new: a merchant, a challenger, then the bank and the bridge to the next island.

Winning. Every level has its own goal: eat enough apples, reach a score, survive, find keys and a gate, press plates, catch a thief, or find a relic hidden somewhere in an endless maze.

Bosses. The first fight, outlast it until its stamina runs out. The second, wear it out and bite its body while it's exhausted to crack its silver armor. The third, do the same to its red hearts.

Your kit. Gear wears out, spells (Space) and snacks get used up, familiars stay with you, and a rune burns up as a level starts. Every item has a strength and a weakness. Swap them between levels from the Satchel.

Money. Each island has its own currency for its own upgrade tree, and every upgrade works everywhere. Banks trade money down to the island before, or into gems for skins and themes.

Home base. The tree on every island: upgrades, your satchel, achievements, skins and themes.

About

An original game. The story, art, soundtrack and narration were all made for it. Thank you for playing.

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD: steer the snake (either can be turned off in Settings)

Space: cast your spell during a level

Esc or P: pause

Touch: swipe on the board or use the on-screen pad; the extra button casts your spell

The map: drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a spot on the path
